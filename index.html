Let's be clear

This is not an AI club.

No chatbots, no prompt engineering, no hype. Blueprint is about the physical, tangible side of technology: the machines, networks, sensors and infrastructure that actually run the world around us, and the people who build them.

Hardware Energy & infrastructure Sensors & mapping Agritech Manufacturing Networks

You belong here

Built for people who've never been in the room.

Most of us study how technology is governed, funded and argued about without ever seeing it made. Blueprint is where that changes. You don't need a technical background, a laptop full of code, or any idea what an API is. You just need to be curious.

  • Never coded? Perfect.Everything starts from the basics, explained by people who do this for a living.
  • Politics, econ, law, history: all in.The best questions about technology come from people outside it.
  • Already technical? Come teach us.If you can solder, write code or handle GIS, there's a lot you can help build.
  • English, français, or both.Come as you are, in whichever language you're comfortable in.

What we'll do together

A year of getting your hands on real tech.

Three things, all year long. Come to whatever fits your schedule.

Company visits

Go inside the companies around Reims and see how their systems actually run, from vineyards using remote sensing to startups at the Bezannes hub.

Hands-on projects

Work in mixed teams on real engineering problems from local companies, then present what you built. No prior skills required, just willingness to try.

Engineer conversations

Informal talks with the people who design and build these systems. Ask them anything, including the questions you'd be too shy to ask in class.

Why Reims

The tech is already here. We're just opening the door.

Reims is home to Le Village by CA, an open-innovation hub backed by Crédit Agricole Nord Est and sixteen local companies and institutions. The businesses around the city work on precision agriculture, supply-chain traceability and climate modelling. Until now, no student group on campus has connected us to any of it.

Questions

Things people ask.

Do I need to know how to code?+

Not at all. Most people joining have never written a line of code, and that's exactly who this is for. Everything starts from the basics.

How much time does it take?+

As much as you want. Come to the visits and talks that interest you. If you want to go deeper, join a project team or help run things.

Does it cost anything?+

No. Everything is free to attend.

Is it in English or French?+

Both. The campus is international, and so are we.

Can students from other schools join?+

Yes. Engineering students from URCA and EiSINe are very welcome on project teams. Mixing backgrounds is the whole point.

Is this an AI club?+

No, and on purpose. Blueprint is about hardware, infrastructure, energy and the physical systems behind things. You won't spend a single session on chatbots.

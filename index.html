The gap

We spend two years studying how technology is governed, funded and regulated, without ever seeing it built.

There is a 7,000 m² innovation hub twenty minutes from campus in Bezannes, backed by Crédit Agricole Nord Est and sixteen local companies and institutions. The firms around Reims work on remote sensing, supply-chain traceability, anti-counterfeiting and climate modelling. No association on this campus has ever organised anything with any of them.

What we do

01

Site visits

Inside the technical operations of companies around Reims. Working sites, not corporate showrooms.

02

Engineer talks

The people who actually build and maintain these systems, on campus, explaining how they work.

03

Build weekend

A partner company sets a real engineering problem. Mixed teams work on it across two days and present the results.

Who it's for

Anyone, whatever you study.

No technical background is required and nothing here assumes you can code. If you can already solder, write firmware or handle GIS, there is plenty to do — but the point of this is that most of us can't yet.

Students from EiSINe and URCA are welcome on the team. The project is led from Sciences Po Reims but the work is better with engineers in the room.
